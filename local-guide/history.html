The town of Fuquay, while unassuming at first, actually has some very interesting historical facts. From a spring with healing properties, to being the home of famous internet personalities, Rhett and Link.

Fuquay-Varina wasn't originally known as "Fuquay-Varina". It was originally a small farming town known as Sippihaw, named after the original native american tribe that inhabited the area. It is worth noting though that there are no historical records of a tribe called Sippihaw living in the area. There are however records of a tribe called "Susippihaw" in the region, of which Sippihaw was likely a mishearing or mispelling of the original tribe's name.
