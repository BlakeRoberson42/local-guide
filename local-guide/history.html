The town of Fuquay, while unassuming at first, actually has some very interesting historical facts. From a spring with healing properties, to once being the home of famous internet personalities, Rhett and Link.

Fuquay-Varina wasn't originally known as "Fuquay-Varina". It was originally a small farming town known as Sippihaw, named after the original native american tribe that inhabited the area. It is worth noting though that there are no historical records of a tribe called Sippihaw living in the area. There are however records of a tribe called "Susippihaw" in the region, of which Sippihaw was likely a mishearing or mispelling of the original tribe's name.

Eventually, a frenchman named William Fuquay came and settled here, purchasing one thousand acres of land. His son Stephen Fuquay was plowing the family tobaco farm when he came across a spring. Originally it was used for drinking water, but Stephen later began claiming that the waters of the spring had supernatural healing properties. Word spread and eventually tourists were coming to try a drink of the spring which was of course later marketed as an attraction. With the fame of the spring and the Fuquay surname, Sippihaw was later named "Fuquay Springs".

Later on, the civil war broke out. A young soldier recieved a morale boosting letter which was signed with the pen name, Varina. This was actually a woman by the name Virginia Avery who lived in Fayettville. After the war, our young soldier found his pen pal and they fell in love. Though he now knew her real name, he continued to affectionately call her Varina. In 1880 he became the postmaster of the town's new post office and named it Varina in her honor. A community began growing just south of Fuquay Springs around the Varina post office and the couple's general store which was named Varina Mercantile. Eventually this community became a town of its own and adopted the Varina name.

Fuquay Springs and Varina both became major trading hubs for southern Wake County. They were close knit as they bordered one another tightly that residents of both towns would frequently attend the same churches, work on the same fields, children would play with one another across towns, and as their shared financial growth continued, they finally realized their common goals and familial community. With this, in 1963 they decided to merge the two towns into one and call it Fuquay-Varina.

Fun fact, famous internet personalities Rhett and Link of the show Good Mythical Morning used to live in Fuquay-Varina. They grew up nearby and in 2010 they opened a studio in Fuquay-Varina where they made content in their early years. Eventually they moved studios to Rhett's old house which is near the original studio, also in Fuquay. These locaions still stand and locals will pass by to get a little glimpse of internet history.
