The Social Board & JT's Creamery

The social board is a small board game shop located inside of another shop called JT's Creamery. Here you can sit down with a cup or cone of ice cream and play some board games with friends, choosing from the store's free to play selection. If you see a game you like, you can find it on the shelves for purchase so you can bring it home with you.

Aviator Brewing

Aviator Brewing is a company with several different locations in downtown Fuquay. The Aviator Brewing Tap House & Kitchen is located in the old Fuquay train station on East Broad Street where people often enjoy a nice meal, grab some drinks and chat with locals. Across the street is Aviator Pizzeria where you can order pizza and sandwiches while sitting on a second story outdoor balcony. Further down the street on North Street is the Aviator Hangar Bar where live bands often play and new beers are released. They also have a real plane you can view just outside.
